About Me

My background

My name is August, and I'm a web developer based in Copenhagen. My background started in multimedia design, but over the years I became increasingly interested in programming and web development.

I've always enjoyed working creatively with technology. Skateboarding has been a big part of my life since I was a kid, and through skating I became interested in photography, videography and eventually digital design.

What I care about

I'm especially interested in creating websites that are accessible, useful and easy to understand. During my education I've become more aware of how small design and development decisions can affect whether people are able to use a digital product.

I enjoy the combination of visual design and programming, and I like working on projects where functionality and user experience are equally important.

Outside development

Outside of web development, I'm still very much into skateboarding, music and videography. These interests have influenced the way I approach design and creative projects, and they continue to be a big part of how I spend my time.
